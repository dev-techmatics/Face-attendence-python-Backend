Download for Excel is a different, fuller thing: a workbook with one sheet per month, each in its own colour, holding one row per employee per date in employee-id order — the check-in, the check-out, the hours, and which half of a pair is missing where one is. Days nobody turned up are rows too, with the times left blank: those are the ones a loss-of-pay check is looking for, and a file that left them out would mean reading the gaps between dates to find them. The Reason column is left empty on purpose — it is yours to write in. A check-out that lands the next morning carries its date, so a night shift reads correctly.
