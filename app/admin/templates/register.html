Hours are the time between each in and the out that followed it, so a lunch break punched out and back in is not counted as time at work. Missed means they checked in and never checked out, and it is now too late for one to arrive; no hours are shown for those days and no leaving time is invented, so somebody has to decide what should have been recorded. While a person is still inside, the out column stays empty even if they went out and came back earlier — the tag says when they last came in. Failed attempts are not here at all, because a failure has no other half to pair with; they are on the Every attempt tab.
