One row per check-in, paired with the check-out that closed it, so somebody who went out at lunch and came back has two rows for that day. Hours are measured from the clock and never from the calendar: a night shift that starts before midnight and ends after it is one row, counted in full, against the date it started — the +1 beside a check-out means it landed on a later date, and hovering it shows which. Missed means they checked in and never checked out and it is now too late for one to arrive; no hours are shown and no leaving time is invented, so somebody has to decide what should have been recorded. Failed attempts are not here at all, because a failure has no other half to pair with{% if is_admin %}; they are on the Every attempt tab{% endif %}.
